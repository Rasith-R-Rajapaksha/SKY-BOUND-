Frequently Addressed Protocol

How quickly can the pilots be on-site? 24 - 48 HRS

For local shoots in LA or Atlanta, we maintain a 24-hour dispatch readiness. For cross-border assignments, our gear packs conform to airline lithium battery travel guidelines for rapid flyaway deployments.

What is the advantage of the iPhone 17 Pro Max rig? CINEMA LOG

Sachith Malshan pairs the iPhone 17 Pro Max with a counterweighted 3-axis motorized gimbal and captures in uncompressed Apple Log ProRes 4K directly to external high-speed SSDs. This offers ultra-light agility impossible with cumbersome cinema cameras while retaining full grading latitude.

How does dual-operator drone flight work? DUAL CONTROL

Rasith Rajapaksha navigates the flight path with precision proximity maneuvers while Dineth Ekanayaka controls the independent gimbal framing and live telemetry feed. This separation ensures extreme kinetic safety and flawless cinematic composition.

What is needed for Canon XC60 Live Streams? FB / YT READY

We bring complete multi-cam hardware encoders and bonded cellular backhauls. We output crystal clear 4K downsampled 1080p60 feeds directly to YouTube Live, Facebook Live, or private corporate distribution streams with dedicated mic routing.
